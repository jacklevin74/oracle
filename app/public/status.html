3. What you receive

    
Streams send every chosen asset when you connect, then only assets whose price or status changed,
      batched at most every 250 ms. Each message looks like:

    
{"type":"prices","timestamp":"2026-09-30T21:58:30.638Z","data":[
  {"symbol":"GOLD","price":4161.6998,"status":"ok","sourcesUsed":5,"sourcesTotal":5,
   "updatedAt":"2026-09-30T21:58:30.545Z"}
]}

    

      

        
FieldMeaning

        
          
priceUSD price: median of the exchanges that agree. null when no exchange has fresh data.

          
statusok = enough exchanges agree · degraded = fewer than the asset's minimum · down = no fresh data.

          
sourcesUsed / sourcesTotalExchanges contributing to the price, out of those configured for the asset.

          
updatedAtTime of the freshest exchange quote used.

          
sourcesOnly with detail=true: each exchange's market, USD price and state (used, outlier, stale, no_data).

        
      

    


    
4. Endpoints

    

      

        
EndpointReturns

        
          
GET /v1/stream/prices?symbols=…Server-Sent Events stream of the chosen assets. Unknown symbols return 400 with the list of available ones.

          
WS /v1/wsWebSocket. Send {"op":"subscribe","symbols":[…]} or {"op":"unsubscribe",…} any time; {"op":"list"} shows your subscription; {"op":"ping"} gets a pong.

          
GET /v1/pricesCurrent snapshot of every asset, with per-exchange detail.

          
GET /v1/prices/:symbolCurrent snapshot of one asset, e.g. /v1/prices/btc.

          
GET /v1/tokensAvailable symbols.

          
GET /healthService and exchange-stream health.

        
      

    

    
Limits: 20 concurrent streams per IP (SSE and WebSocket combined) and 600 HTTP requests per minute per IP.
      Streams over the limit get HTTP 503. Clients that fall far behind are disconnected, so reconnect on close.

  


  

    Prices are the median of fresh top-of-book mid prices across exchange websocket streams,
    with sources outside each asset's deviation limit rejected. USDT-quoted markets are converted
    with the live USDT/USD rate. Click an asset for per-source detail.
